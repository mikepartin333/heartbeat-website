Heartbeat Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Heartbeat is a personal-use notification system operated for private reminders and alerts. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be used by Heartbeat and how that information is handled.

Information used by Heartbeat

Heartbeat may use names, mobile telephone numbers, reminder information, event details, notification history, and technical information needed to operate the system and deliver requested messages.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate Heartbeat, maintain authorized recipient lists, generate personal reminders or alerts, deliver requested notifications, and troubleshoot the system.

SMS consent information

When an individual voluntarily authorizes Heartbeat to send SMS notifications, information related to that authorization may be maintained by the system owner for the purpose of documenting and managing the authorized notification relationship.

SMS consent information and mobile telephone numbers are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Heartbeat does not use purchased or scraped telephone-number lists for SMS notifications.

Mobile information

Mobile telephone numbers may be processed by the SMS messaging provider solely as necessary to deliver authorized and requested messages.

The SMS messaging provider may process message and telephone number information according to its own terms and privacy policies.

SMS messages

Recipients may receive messages when a configured personal reminder or authorized alert occurs. Messages may include appointment reminders, scheduled task reminders, household alerts, weather notifications, computer alerts, or home-automation notifications.

Message frequency varies according to personal events and system alerts.

Message and data rates may apply.

Voluntary participation

SMS notification is voluntary. Recipients are added to the Heartbeat notification system only after they have authorized the system owner to send personal reminders and alerts to their mobile telephone number.

Recipients may withdraw their authorization and stop receiving SMS notifications at any time.

Data retention

Information may be retained on the owner's computer or other systems used to operate Heartbeat for as long as reasonably necessary for reminders, records, troubleshooting, consent management, or system operation.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect Heartbeat data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party services

Heartbeat may use service providers, including an SMS messaging provider, to deliver requested notifications. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Heartbeat does not authorize third-party service providers to use recipient mobile telephone numbers for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Heartbeat system or its use changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about the Heartbeat notification system or requests to stop receiving notifications may be directed to the system owner.
