Heartbeat Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Heartbeat is a personal-use notification system operated for private reminders and alerts. This policy explains how information used by the system is handled.

Information used by Heartbeat

The system may store names, telephone numbers, reminder information, event details, notification history, and technical information needed to operate the service and deliver messages.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate Heartbeat, maintain authorized recipient lists, generate personal reminders or alerts, deliver requested notifications, and troubleshoot the system.

Mobile information

Heartbeat does not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile telephone numbers may be processed by the SMS messaging provider solely as necessary to deliver requested messages.

SMS messages

Recipients may receive messages when a configured reminder or alert occurs. Message frequency varies according to personal events and system alerts.

Message and data rates may apply.

Data retention

Information may be retained on the owner's computer or other systems used to operate Heartbeat for as long as reasonably necessary for reminders, records, troubleshooting, or system operation.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect Heartbeat data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party services

Heartbeat may use service providers, including an SMS messaging provider, to deliver requested notifications. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Heartbeat system or its use changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
