SMS NOTIFICATION PROGRAM

Heartbeat SMS Notifications

Heartbeat is a personal notification system that sends non-promotional SMS reminders and alerts to individuals who have voluntarily authorized the system to contact them.

What Heartbeat sends

  • Personal appointment and calendar reminders
  • Reminders about scheduled tasks or events
  • Household alerts, such as a detected water leak
  • Computer, home-automation, or equipment alerts
  • Weather-related reminders and notifications
  • Other personal alerts specifically requested or authorized by the recipient

Who sends the messages?

Messages are generated and sent by the Heartbeat personal assistant system on behalf of its owner. Heartbeat is a personal-use notification system and is not a business marketing or advertising service.

Who receives the messages?

Messages are sent only to the system owner and other individuals who have voluntarily authorized Heartbeat to send them SMS notifications. Authorized recipients may include family members or other people personally known to and authorized by the system owner.

How SMS consent works

1. Invitation

The system owner asks an individual whether they would like to receive personal Heartbeat reminders and alerts by SMS.

2. Voluntary authorization

The individual voluntarily agrees to receive the notifications and provides their mobile telephone number directly to the system owner for this purpose.

3. Recipient is added

The system owner adds the individual's telephone number to the Heartbeat notification list only after receiving that permission.

4. Confirmation

After SMS notifications are enabled, the recipient may receive a confirmation message explaining that Heartbeat notifications are active and providing STOP and HELP instructions.

There is no purchased telephone-number list, no scraped telephone-number list, and no automatic enrollment of recipients.

Heartbeat does not use this SMS program to send advertising, promotional offers, or unsolicited commercial messages.

Example consent language

I agree to receive personal SMS reminders and alerts from Heartbeat at the mobile number I provide. These messages may include appointment reminders, scheduled task reminders, household alerts, weather notifications, and computer or home-automation alerts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. I understand that I can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Example confirmation message

Heartbeat notifications are now enabled for this number. You will receive personal reminders and alerts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on the recipient's personal reminders, appointments, household events, weather notifications, and system alerts. Heartbeat does not send messages according to a promotional marketing schedule.

Opt out

Recipients may stop receiving Heartbeat SMS notifications at any time.

Replying STOP requests that SMS notifications cease. Other standard carrier-supported opt-out commands may also be recognized.

Help

Recipients may reply HELP where supported by the messaging service or contact the Heartbeat system owner directly for assistance.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Recipients should contact their mobile carrier for information about their individual messaging plan.

Privacy

Heartbeat uses telephone numbers only for authorized notification purposes. Telephone numbers are not purchased from third parties or used for advertising.

See the Privacy Policy for additional information about how Heartbeat handles information.
