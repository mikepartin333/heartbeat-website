SMS NOTIFICATION PROGRAM

Heartbeat SMS Notifications

Heartbeat sends personal, non-promotional text messages to recipients who have authorized the system to contact them.

What messages may contain

  • Personal appointment and calendar reminders
  • Reminders about scheduled tasks or events
  • Household alerts, such as a detected water leak
  • Computer, home-automation, or system alerts
  • Weather-related reminders and notifications
  • Links to relevant information, such as weather information, personal dashboards, appointment details, or household alerts

Who receives messages?

Messages are intended only for the system owner and other individuals who have expressly authorized Heartbeat to send them notifications. This may include immediate family members or other personally authorized recipients.

How recipients authorize SMS

Heartbeat uses a direct, person-to-person consent process. There is no public marketing signup form and no purchased telephone-number list.

Before a recipient is added to the Heartbeat SMS notification system, the system owner obtains the recipient's direct permission to receive personal reminders and alerts at the provided mobile telephone number.

Only recipients who have authorized the system are added to the notification list. The system owner maintains the list of authorized recipients.

Recipients may withdraw their permission at any time by contacting the system owner or by replying STOP where supported.

Consent message

When SMS notification service is enabled for an authorized recipient, the following type of confirmation may be provided:

Heartbeat SMS notifications have been enabled for this number. You will receive personal reminders and alerts. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies according to personal reminders, appointments, household events, weather notifications, and system alerts. There is no fixed marketing or promotional messaging schedule.

Consent is not required for marketing

Heartbeat does not send advertising, promotional offers, marketing messages, or unsolicited commercial messages. Messages are limited to personal reminders and alerts requested or authorized by the recipient.

Opt-out

Recipients may request removal from the Heartbeat notification list at any time.

Where carrier-supported messaging commands are enabled, replying STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT requests that SMS notifications cease.

Help

For assistance, recipients may contact the Heartbeat system owner directly. Where supported by the messaging configuration, replying HELP may provide assistance.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Recipients should check with their mobile carrier for details about their messaging plan.

Privacy and Terms
